For every type of question or interview in our process, we always score with the same system, so we have consistency across the board. This way, no matter the role, anyone can quickly say a number from 1 to 4 and others will instantly know what that means.

## The 1-4 scoring system

At Distru, we use the **1-4 scoring system**:

1. Strong no hire; examine why the candidate got to this point if needed
2. Neutral no hire; they didn't impress nor disappoint
3. Neutral hire; they did good, but not outstanding
4. Strong hire; we need to reflect on our interview process if we don't close this candidate

There's no middle score, so every score is either a hire or a no hire.

## Choosing a score

General rules when choosing a score:

- A candidate must be at least a 3 to move forward in the process
- If you're stuck between a 2 and a 3, it's a 2 by default
- Not all attributes are equal (e.g. if you really value product sense in a role, then a 3 there overrides a 2 in another category)
